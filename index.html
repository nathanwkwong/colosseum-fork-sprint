Two collusion systems

Colosseum and Audit the Whisper are separate repositories. They do not share a runner, a dataset, or a detector.

How the two systems differ

Colosseum watches task outcomes on a communication graph: reward, regret, and whether a lie or a suspicion shows up in a survey. Audit the Whisper watches the text channel itself: a planted bit string, auditor edits, and a calibrated union of detectors.
